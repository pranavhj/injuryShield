## Tips

- **Do 3-4 connection requests per day, not all at once** — looks more natural
- **Personalize the first line** if their profile gives you a hook (specific sport, specific clinic achievement)
- **Check "People Also Viewed"** on each profile — LinkedIn shows similar profiles (other clinic owners)
- **Join the group** "Private Practice Physical Therapy" on LinkedIn — members are your exact target
- **If someone declines or ignores:** don't follow up more than once. Move to the next person.
- **Target: 12 requests → 8 accepts → 5 interviews** (reasonable conversion for warm local outreach)
