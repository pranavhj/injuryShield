## Our Target (TAM/SAM/SOM estimates — needs refinement)

**TAM (Total Addressable Market):**
- $2.66B injury prediction wearable sensor market (2026)
- Growing at 16.3% CAGR

**SAM (Serviceable Available Market):**
- US high school + college + amateur sports programs using technology
- ~30,000 college programs + ~20,000 competitive high schools + millions of individual athletes
- Estimate: if 5% of programs adopt at $500/team/year = $125M

**SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market — Year 1-3):**
- Individual runners/athletes: 1,000 units × $105 = $105K
- College programs: 50 programs × $2,000/program = $100K
- Realistic Year 1-3 revenue: $200K-$500K

## Key Market Drivers
1. 41% of new sports wearables in 2025 incorporated on-device AI/ML (up from 18% in 2022)
2. Training load & injury-risk management = 36% of player tracking demand
3. NCAA 2026 wearable guidelines creating urgency for data governance
4. IPL/cricket franchises hiring full-time analytics staff (India opportunity)
5. Cost of professional sports injuries (NFL teams lose $10M+/year to preventable injuries)
